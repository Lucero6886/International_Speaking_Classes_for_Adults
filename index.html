Scenario 1: The Deadline Approach

Situation: You cannot finish your current project on time and need to ask your boss for an extension.

🧑‍💼 Role A (Employee) Explain why you are running late and politely ask for more time to ensure high quality.
👔 Role B (Manager) Ask for specific reasons. Be slightly strict about the schedule, but eventually agree to the new deadline.
  • "I apologize for the inconvenience, but..."
  • "I underestimated how time-consuming this would be."
  • "Could we possibly push the deadline to Friday?"
  • "I want to make sure the final result is perfect."

Scenario 2: The Busy Friend

Situation: You want to hang out with your friend this weekend, but their schedule is extremely packed.

👋 Role A (You) Suggest a specific time and a quick activity (e.g., grabbing coffee, a short walk).
📅 Role B (Friend) Look at your imaginary schedule. Decline the first offer, but try to squeeze them in for 1 hour later.
  • "Are you free to catch up on Saturday morning?"
  • "Let me check my calendar real quick..."
  • "My schedule is completely booked on Sunday."
  • "Can we squeeze in a quick coffee break around 3 PM?"
