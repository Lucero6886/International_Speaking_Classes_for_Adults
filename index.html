2. Vocabulary 📖

10 useful words & phrases for small talk and networking. Click a card to see an example sentence. (🔊 = say it twice for the class.)

small talk
noun
Light, friendly conversation about simple topics.
"We made small talk about the weather before the meeting started."
break the ice
phrase
To start a conversation in a friendly, relaxed way.
"A simple question about the weekend can break the ice."
networking
noun
Building relationships with people who could help your career.
"She's good at networking — she knows people in every department."
colleague
noun
A person you work with.
"My colleague introduced me to the new client."
touch base
phrasal verb
To contact someone briefly to share information.
"Let's touch base next week about the project."
approachable
adjective
Easy and pleasant to talk to.
"Our new manager is very approachable, so I asked her a few questions."
follow up
phrasal verb
To contact someone again after a first meeting.
"I'll follow up with an email after the conference."
common ground
noun phrase
Shared interests or opinions between people.
"We found some common ground — we both studied in Hanoi."
elevator pitch
noun
A short description of yourself or your work.
"Prepare a 30-second elevator pitch before the networking event."
catch up
phrasal verb
To talk with someone about recent news.
"Let's catch up over coffee sometime this week."

4. Role-Play 🎭

Two real-life work situations. Read your role, then act it out with a partner. Click Show dialogue hints if you need support.

🎬 Scenario 1 — Company networking mixer

You are at an after-work networking event for people in your industry. Approach someone you don't know, introduce yourself, and try to find one thing you have in common before exchanging contact details.

👤 Student A — Guest at the event 👤 Student B — Another guest, different company
  • A: "Hi there! I don't think we've met — I'm ___ from ___."
  • B: "Nice to meet you, ___! I'm ___. I work at ___ as a ___."
  • A: "Oh, interesting! What made you come to this event tonight?"
  • B: "I'm hoping to meet people in ___. How about you?"
  • A: "Same here! It seems we have something in common — we're both interested in ___."
  • B: "That's great. Here's my card — let's connect on LinkedIn and keep in touch."

🎬 Scenario 2 — Coffee break with a new colleague

It's your coffee break. You notice a colleague from another department sitting alone. Start a friendly conversation, ask about their work, and find a topic you can both talk about.

👤 Student A — Employee from Marketing 👤 Student B — New colleague from IT
  • A: "Hi, mind if I join you? I don't think we've officially met."
  • B: "Not at all! I'm ___ from the IT department. I just joined last month."
  • A: "Welcome to the company! How's everything going so far?"
  • B: "Pretty good, thanks. Everyone's been really approachable and helpful."
  • A: "That's great to hear. What do you do exactly in IT?"
  • B: "I mostly work on ___. What about you — what's your role in Marketing?"
