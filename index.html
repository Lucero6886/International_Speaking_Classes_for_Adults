2. Vocabulary 📖

10 useful words & phrases about money. Click a card to see an example sentence. (🔊 = say it twice for the class.)

budget
noun / verb
A plan for how you will spend and save your money.
"I made a monthly budget so I don't overspend."
save up
phrasal verb
To keep money over time for something you want.
"We're saving up for a new motorbike."
spend
verb
To use money to buy or pay for something.
"I spent too much money on clothes last month."
income
noun
The money you earn from work or other sources.
"Her income increased after she got a promotion."
expense
noun
Money that you need to spend on something.
"Rent is my biggest monthly expense."
afford
verb
To have enough money to pay for something.
"We can't afford a new car this year."
in debt
expression
Owing money to someone, such as a bank.
"He got into debt after borrowing money for his business."
discount
noun
A reduction in the usual price of something.
"I got a 20% discount on this jacket."
worth it
expression
Good enough to justify the money, time, or effort spent.
"The trip was expensive, but it was totally worth it."
financial goal
noun phrase
Something related to money that you plan to achieve.
"My financial goal is to buy a house before I'm 35."

4. Role-Play 🎭

Two real-life situations. Read your role, then act it out with a partner. Click Show dialogue hints if you need support.

🎬 Scenario 1 — Planning a shared monthly budget

You and your roommate need to agree on a monthly budget for rent, food, and going out. One of you wants to save more; the other likes to spend on fun. Reach an agreement together.

👤 Student A — Wants to save more 👤 Student B — Likes spending on going out
  • A: "Can we talk about our budget? I think we're spending too much on eating out."
  • B: "Really? I don't think it's that bad — I like going out with friends."
  • A: "I understand, but I'm trying to save for ___. Maybe we could cook at home more?"
  • B: "That's fair. How about we set a budget of ___ VND a month for food?"
  • A: "Sounds good. Let's also split the electricity bill equally."

🎬 Scenario 2 — Asking a bank staff member about savings options

You want to open a savings account. Ask the bank staff about the options available and choose the one that fits your financial goal.

👤 Student A — Customer 👤 Student B — Bank staff
  • B: "Good morning! How can I help you today?"
  • A: "Hi, I'd like to open a savings account. What options do you have?"
  • B: "We have a flexible one with a low interest rate, and a fixed one with a higher rate if you save for ___ months."
  • A: "What would you recommend for someone saving for ___?"
  • B: "I'd recommend the ___ option because ___. What documents do you have with you today?"
