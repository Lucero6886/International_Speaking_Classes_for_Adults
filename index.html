🤝 Teamwork and Sharing Responsibilities

Module 3 · Week 31 — Work, Study, and Personal Development

🎯 Today you will be able to…

2. Vocabulary 📖

10 useful words & phrases. Click a card to see an example sentence. (🔊 = say it twice for the class.)

delegate
verb
To give a task or responsibility to another person.
"A good leader knows when to delegate tasks to the team."
responsibility
noun
A duty or task that you are expected to do.
"Checking the budget is my responsibility this month."
deadline
noun
The date or time by which something must be finished.
"Our deadline for the report is next Friday."
collaborate
verb
To work together with other people on a task.
"We collaborated closely to finish the presentation on time."
compromise
noun
An agreement where each side accepts a little less than they wanted.
"We reached a compromise on the project timeline."
workload
noun
The amount of work a person has to do.
"My workload is heavier this week because two colleagues are on leave."
contribute
verb
To give ideas, effort, or work toward a shared goal.
"Everyone should contribute ideas during the brainstorming session."
conflict
noun
A disagreement or argument between people.
"There was a small conflict about who should lead the project."
feedback
noun
Comments about how well someone did something.
"My manager gave me useful feedback after the presentation."
team player
phrase
Someone who works well and cooperates with others.
"She's a real team player — always ready to help others."

4. Role-Play 🎭

Two real-life situations. Read your role, then act it out with a partner. Click Show dialogue hints if you need support.

🎬 Scenario 1 — Planning a group project at work

You and a colleague need to divide tasks for a new project with a tight deadline in two weeks. Decide who does what and agree on a plan.

👤 Student A — Team leader assigning tasks 👤 Student B — Team member with a busy schedule
  • A: "Let's divide the project into three parts: research, writing, and design. Which one would you like to take?"
  • B: "I'd prefer research, but I'm quite busy with another project this week."
  • A: "That's fine. Could you finish it by next Wednesday instead?"
  • B: "Sure, that works for me. Can someone help me if I fall behind?"
  • A: "Of course. Let's check our progress together on Friday."

🎬 Scenario 2 — Resolving a disagreement about a group project

You and your teammate disagree about how to present a project — one of you prefers a simple written report, the other prefers a creative video. Find a compromise.

👤 Student A — Prefers a simple written report 👤 Student B — Prefers a creative video presentation
  • A: "I think a written report is safer and easier to prepare in time."
  • B: "I understand, but a video would be more interesting for the audience."
  • A: "That's true, but we only have one week left."
  • B: "What if we make a short video and add a one-page summary report too?"
  • A: "That sounds like a good compromise. Let's try that."
